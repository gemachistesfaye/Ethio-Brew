# Ethio-Brew Enterprise Features
Comprehensive Capability List

## 1. Consumer Features
- **Dynamic Marketplace:** Multilingual product discovery.
- **AI Expert:** Real-time coffee sommelier for personalized picks.
- **Origin Explorer:** Interactive map of Ethiopia's coffee regions.
- **Order Timeline:** Live step-by-step tracking from Roasting to Delivery.
- **Social Stories:** Rich localized content about coffee heritage.

## 2. Business Intelligence
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Revenue trends, user growth, and demand heatmaps.
- **Payment Verification:** Digital verification of Telebirr and CBE screenshots.
- **Inventory Control:** Real-time stock management and category organization.

## 3. Communication
- **Telegram Bot:** Real-time business alerts and customer notifications.
- **Email Hub:** Automated order receipts and verification.

## 4. Performance
- **PWA Support:** Installable on Android/iOS with offline capabilities.
- **Low Bandwidth Mode:** Optimized images and lazy loading for Ethiopian networks.
- **Skeleton Loading:** Seamless UI transitions.

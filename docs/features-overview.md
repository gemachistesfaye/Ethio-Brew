# ✨ Ethio-Brew — Features Overview

Ethio-Brew is more than an e-commerce site; it is a cultural and business ecosystem.

## 🛍️ E-Commerce Features
- **Regional Discovery:** Browse coffee by Ethiopia's famous regions (Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Harar).
- **Multilingual Support:** Full native experience in English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromo.
- **Dynamic Cart:** Real-time quantity adjustments and price calculations.
- **Local Payments:** Integrated support for Telebirr, CBE Birr, and mobile banking receipts.

## 📊 Admin Command Center
- **Business Intelligence:** Real-time charts for revenue, orders, and regional demand.
- **Inventory Management:** Full CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) for coffee products.
- **Order Pipeline:** Move orders from "Roasting" to "Delivered" with a single click.
- **User Moderation:** Manage roles and platform access for staff and customers.

## 🤖 AI Intelligence
- **Coffee Sommelier:** AI assistant that recommends blends based on user taste and language.
- **Market Analysis:** AI identifies flavor trends and suggests stock adjustments for Admins.
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Automated tracking of customer satisfaction from reviews.

## 📜 Cultural Content
- **Heritage Stories:** In-depth articles about Kaldi, the history of coffee, and the Buna ceremony.
- **Farmer Spotlights:** Transparent sourcing stories directly from the highlands.
- **Brewing Blog:** Expert guides on Jebena brewing and coffee science.
